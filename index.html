About Me

Hey, I'm Marco.

I'm a 29-year-old surfer, home chef, and self-proclaimed ocean obsessive based out of San Diego, CA. Growing up along the coast, the Pacific wasn't just my playground — it was my classroom.

After spending years in a corporate marketing career that left me feeling disconnected, I quit my job, bought a one-way ticket to Portugal, and surfed my way back to myself. That trip changed everything.

Today I share my journey of intentional living — sustainable food, mindful fitness, slow travel, and the relentless push to protect the ocean we all depend on.
