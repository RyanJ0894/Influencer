About Me

Hey, I'm Marco.

I'm a 29-year-old surfer, athlete, and self-proclaimed ocean obsessive based out of San Diego, CA. Growing up along the coast, the Pacific wasn't just my playground — it was my classroom.

After years in a corporate marketing career that left me feeling disconnected and out of shape, I quit my job, bought a one-way ticket to Portugal, and surfed and trained my way back to myself. That trip changed everything.

Today I share my journey of intentional living — sustainable food, functional fitness, mindful movement, slow travel, and the relentless push to protect the ocean we all depend on.

I don't train to look a certain way. I train so I can paddle out in heavy surf, hike to remote waterfalls, carry my own gear, and feel fully alive in every environment I put myself in.

My approach is functional, outdoor-first, and built around movement patterns that translate directly to real-world performance — calisthenics, breathwork, mobility, and cold exposure.
